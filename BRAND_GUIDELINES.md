## 4. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

Nexus Royale communicates with modern, technical precision combined with high-impact gaming authority.

### 4.1 Font Families
- **Display & Headings:** `Outfit`, `Inter`, `Cabinet Grotesk`, or `-apple-system, system-ui, sans-serif`
  - *Weights:* 800 (ExtraBold), 900 (Black)
  - *Letter Spacing:* `+0.04em` to `+0.08em`
  - *Transform:* UPPERCASE for titles, hero statements, and button CTAs.
- **Body & Controls:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Text`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif
  - *Weights:* 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 700 (Bold)
  - *Line Height:* `1.5` to `1.6`
- **Telemetry & Numerical Data:** `JetBrains Mono`, `Space Mono`, `Menlo`, monospace
  - *Use:* Player tags (`#Y0JJY80`), trophy counters (`14,000 🏆`), in-game card IDs, elixir costs, timestamps.
