# Nexus Royale — Brand Guidelines (V1.0)
*Modeled 1:1 on the Supercell Official Brand Standard*

---

## 1. Philosophy & Brand Purpose

> **"We want our brand identity to be as high quality as the games we celebrate, which is why we have put together this brand standard."**

Our design is **supportive rather than expressive**, to give room for our players' gameplay, live battle telemetry, and card artwork to shine. Inside our Brand Guidelines you’ll find written, visual, and graphic elements that comprise our brand to ensure consistency across all web applications, companion tools, content channels, and esports broadcasts.

---

## 2. Logo System

### Core Mark
The Nexus Royale mark consists of the stacked geometric monogram:
```
N E X
U S •
R O Y
```
followed by the primary wordmark **NEXUS ROYALE**.

### Quick Guide
- **Monochrome Integrity:** Use the logo only in pure black (`#000000`) or pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Preferred Backgrounds:** Black background is preferred; use white background if needed.
- **Space Protection:** No typography, graphics, icons, or badges should infringe on its designated boundary.
- **On Imagery:** When placed over arena photography or gameplay footage, the white logo is mandatory.
- **On Colored Backgrounds:** When a colored background is required, use the white logo.
- **Integrity Rule:** The logo should never be stretched, recolored, re-typeset, or altered with unapproved drop shadows.

### Clear Space
In order to make our logo identifiable, it must always be given space to breathe. Breathing is very important.
* **Calculation:** The minimum clear space surrounding the mark is measured by the **height of the letter X** in the monogram.

### Location & Placement
* **Primary Location:** Centered on the page or canvas.
* **Secondary Location:** Bottom-left or bottom-right corner.

### Sizing Scale
* **Primary:** Logo height is **⅓ of short length** of the artboard.
* **Secondary:** If the primary rule makes the logo feel disproportionately large, use **¼ of short length**.
* **Small (Stationery & Favicons):** Logo height is **⅛ of short length**.
* **Large (Hero Banners & Billboards):** Logo height is **½ of page length**, where a dominant statement logo is required.

## 3. Typography

Nexus Royale uses two distinct brand typefaces that create a disciplined, high-performance aesthetic:

### 1. Nexus Headline
* **Characteristics:** Bold, geometric, uppercase, and assertive. Used for headlines, major scoreboards, card titles, and callouts.
* **Weights:**
  - *Primary:* Nexus Headline Heavy (Black / 900)
  - *Secondary:* Nexus Headline Bold (Bold / 700)
* **Specifications:**
  - **Casing:** Always ALL CAPITALS
  - **Scale:** Always large and commanding
  - **Leading:** 10% of type size
  - **Kerning:** Optical
  - **Tracking:** -15 (tightened for impact)
  - **Alignment:** Left-aligned or centered

### 2. Nexus Text
* **Characteristics:** Clean, highly legible, humanistic sans-serif paired for subheads, player tags, statistics, and body copy. Highly functional and timeless.
* **Weights:** Regular (400), Medium (500), and Bold (700).
* **Specifications:**
  - **Casing:** Sentence case or all capitals for field labels
  - **Leading:** 45% of type size
  - **Kerning:** Optical
  - **Tracking:** 0
  - **Alignment:** Left or center aligned

## 4. Color Palette

### Primary Colors (The 70% Bedrock)
These are our hero colors and the foundation of our brand. Black and white signify precision, focus, and analytical clarity, while giving vibrant Clash Royale card art room to shine.

| Color Name | HEX | CMYK | Pantone (Coated) | Pantone (Uncoated) | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nexus Black** | `#000000` | 40C 30M 30Y 100K | PMS Black 6 C | PMS Neutral Black U | Primary background & type |
| **Nexus White** | `#FFFFFF` | 0C 0M 0Y 0K | — | — | Primary canvas & reversed type |

### Accent Colors (The 30% Palette)
Our accent colors are fun and bright, sharing a complementary hue with the official Supercell palette. Use them thoughtfully — **ideally one accent color at a time** to prevent visual noise.

| Color Name | HEX | CMYK | Pantone | Priority | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Royale Blue** | `#2D85F3` | 80C 40M 0Y 0K | PMS 2727C | **Primary** | Victory states, primary CTA, links, trophies |
| **Royale Green** | `#21C49F` | 70C 0M 50Y 0K | PMS 3265C | **Primary** | Watch buttons, positive trade, win indicators |
| **Elixir Pink** | `#FB62AA` | 0C 80M 0Y 0K | PMS 212C | **Secondary** | Elixir costs, notifications, loss indicators |
| **Crown Yellow** | `#F7DB1C` | 5C 10M 90Y 0K | PMS 109C | **Secondary** | Gold currency, active deck ribbon, star tiers |

### Supporting Grays
Supporting grays establish visual hierarchy across cards, modal backdrops, and secondary metrics:

* **Dark Gray:** `#191919` — Surface cards, table alternating rows, secondary panels.
* **Medium Gray:** `#666666` — Borders, divider rules, neutral labels.
* **Gray:** `#CCCCCC` — Unselected tabs, inactive button borders.
* **Light Gray:** `#F2F2F2` — Subtle stone tinting, high-contrast badges on light mode.

## 5. Gradients

Gradients are applied sparingly to bring focus to specific data points (e.g. radial win rate rings, timeline bars, and tournament charts):

* **Gradient A (Telemetry):** `linear-gradient(90deg, #2D85F3 0%, #21C49F 100%)`  
  *Royale Blue to Royale Green* — Represents synergy, high win probability, and optimal trades.
* **Gradient B (Battle Intensity):** `linear-gradient(90deg, #2D85F3 0%, #FB62AA 100%)`  
  *Royale Blue to Elixir Pink* — Represents live action, overtime excitement, and 2v2 medals.

---

## 6. Color Usage Ratio: 70 / 30 Rule

```
[===================== 70% Nexus Black & White =====================][=== 30% Accents ===]
```

* **70% Primary Foundation:** Black and white form 70% of any composition, ensuring clean whitespace, readability, and contrast.
* **30% Accent Energy:** Royale Blue, Royale Green, Elixir Pink, and Crown Yellow provide intentional highlights, indicators, and interactive affordances.

## 7. Fan Content & Legal Compliance Standard

Nexus Royale is built in strict adherence to the **Supercell Fan Content Policy**:
1. **Disclaimer Notice:** Every official touchpoint (web footer, social post caption, media kit) must feature:
   > *"This content is not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored, or specifically approved by Supercell and Supercell is not responsible for it. For more information see Supercell's Fan Content Policy: www.supercell.com/fan-content-policy."*
2. **Distinct Identity:** The Nexus Royale logo and crest must never be confusingly similar to the official Clash Royale logo, and companion tools must be clearly identified as fan-created.
