BRAND GUIDELINES

We want our brand identity to be as high quality as the games we celebrate, which is why we have put together this brand standard.

Our design is supportive rather than expressive, to give room for live player telemetry, battle analytics, and Clash Royale's legendary gameplay to shine. Inside our Brand Guidelines you’ll find written, visual and graphic elements that comprise our brand. The following pages give guidance for using them in a consistent, distinctly Nexus Royale way.

Logo

The logo is our most valuable asset. It symbolizes our mission: uniting Clash Royale competitive players with instantaneous live telemetry and elite AI tactical tools. It should never be recreated or altered.

QUICK GUIDE
  • Use logo only in black or white
  • Black background is preferred, use white background if needed
  • No type or other elements should infringe on its space
  • On image, white logo is preferred
  • When color background is needed, use white logo

Typography

Nexus Royale has two distinct brand typefaces, Nexus Headline and Nexus Text. Paired together they create a distinct look that feels uniquely ours while honoring Supercell's playful aesthetic.

NEXUS HEADLINE

Bold and geometric. Nexus Headline is used in headlines, scoreboards, and larger text — in any type that needs attention: titles, key metrics, or call outs.

NEXUS TEXT

Nexus Text is paired for best legibility for smaller text — subheads, player tags, telemetry metrics, and body copy. It’s highly functional and timeless.

Accent Colors

We want our accent colors to be fun and bright, just like Clash Royale. Accent colors should be used to highlight text, telemetry metrics, or to create UI elements.

While the colors are not game specific, they share a complementary hue to the official arena palette. Accent colors should be used thoughtfully — ideally one accent color at a time.
