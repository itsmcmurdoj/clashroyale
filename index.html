👑 Feature Rarity vs Champions
Champions (e.g. Boss Bandit, Goblinstein) are native card rarities. Heroes are regular base cards (e.g. Knight, Ice Wizard, Valkyrie) that gain active manual abilities when assigned to your deck's Hero Slot.
🪙 Arena 5 & Hero Coins
Unlocked immediately at Arena 5. Players use deterministic Hero Coins (capped at 200). Any overflow automatically converts 1:1 into Gems with zero random lootboxes.
⚖️ Single-Use Ability Rule
Following the August 26, 2026 balance update, all Hero & Champion abilities are strictly single-use per arena deployment (with the sole exception of the Boss Bandit, who retains multi-use dash cooldowns).
